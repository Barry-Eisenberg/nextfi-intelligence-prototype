The Tension To Watch

Move fast, says one source.
Speed already failed, says another.

Pressure to accelerate

Goldman: most capital, infrastructure and M&A has not yet been deployed. Positioning now matters.

BCG: the window is open because most competitors have not moved past pilots.

vs
Evidence against speed

BCG: two years of pilots, underwhelming ROI. Half-measures lead to pilot purgatory.

McKinsey: 44% of organizations that reached reinvention still say they are not ready.

NextFi assessment

The resolution is depth, not breadth. Concentrate on two or three end-to-end redesigns with named P&L targets. Fast pilots without governance become expensive liabilities.

13 / 15
The Execution Path

Three phases. Named exits.

1
Diagnose & prioritise Weeks 1–6

Place activity honestly against the horizons. Score organizational readiness separately — and give it an owner.

Exit: named workflows with baseline economics · a readiness score with an owner · a documented data-gap inventory.
2
Redesign & govern Weeks 6–16

Redesign around what AI makes possible, not existing process maps. Build governance in from day one — retrofitting is exponentially harder.

Exit: two or three workflows redesigned with P&L targets · a signed human-in-the-loop boundary map · an MRM protocol covering agentic systems.
3
Produce & position Months 4–12

Move to production with workflow-level metrics, not tool-level ones. Tie outcomes to scorecards. Engage the capital-formation opportunity.

Exit: workflow-level P&L attribution · AI outcomes in performance reviews · a documented position on settlement infrastructure.
14 / 15
The Bottom Line

This is a program design problem,
not a technology selection problem.

The firms winning this transition are not running more pilots. They are running fewer, better-designed production systems — governance built in from the start, data architecture treated as infrastructure, workforce transition managed as a program risk.

And one dimension the three sources do not address: the settlement layer is moving too. Institutions designing AI operating models today without accounting for programmable settlement are building toward a second redesign within 24 to 36 months.
